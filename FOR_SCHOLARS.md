# The Biblical Consultant — A Research Tool for the Bible

## What it is

The **Biblical Consultant** is a research tool that lets you ask questions about the Bible and receive answers grounded in the **original Hebrew and Greek texts**, each with its exact book, chapter, and verse citation.

It contains the complete Bible in its original languages:
- the **Hebrew Old Testament** (the Westminster Leningrad Codex),
- the **Greek New Testament** (the SBL Greek New Testament), and
- the **Deuterocanonical books** (Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1–2 Maccabees, and more) in their Greek Septuagint form.

That is over 35,000 verses, searchable by meaning. Ask a question in plain English (or in Hebrew or Greek), and it retrieves the actual verses that answer it, with references you can verify against the original text.

It is not a chatbot that paraphrases the Bible from memory or from a translation. It is a retrieval tool that grounds every answer in the original languages.

## How to use it

The tool works through **Hermes** — an AI assistant that runs on your computer. You do not need to know anything about computers. You just talk to Hermes in plain English, the way you would talk to a colleague.

### Getting started (one time only)

The first time, the tool needs to be set up. This is a one-time thing, and it is easiest if a friend or colleague who is comfortable with computers does it for you — it takes about a minute. They will:

1. Open Hermes and paste in one line of text (the "install" line).
2. Tell Hermes: *"Set up the Biblical consultant."*

Hermes then does everything else on its own — it installs what it needs, downloads the Bible in its original languages, and builds the search index. This takes a few hours the first time, but it happens in the background and you only ever do it once.

### Asking questions (every day)

Once it is set up, you simply ask Hermes questions in plain English, like:

> "What does the Hebrew word *bara* mean in Genesis 1?"
> "What does John mean by *logos* in the prologue?"
> "What does the Bible say about polygamy?"

And you will receive an answer that quotes the original Hebrew or Greek, with the exact citation (for example, *Genesis 1:1* or *John 1:1*), so you can look it up yourself in the original text.

## What it offers

- **The complete Bible in its original languages** — Hebrew Old Testament, Greek New Testament, and the Deuterocanonical books
- **Exact citations** for every verse, so you can verify against the original text
- **The ability to examine the original words** — Hebrew and Greek terms, with their nuances, not just a translation
- **No technical knowledge needed** — you just talk to Hermes in plain English
